I am interested in understanding how theoretical descriptions of physical systems translate into their behaviour in the real world. My research interests lie at the interface of physical chemistry, materials science and device physics, particularly in using experimental approaches to probe this connection—from molecular and material properties to device-level phenomena—and in exploring how such fundamental understanding can guide the development of new materials and functional technologies.
