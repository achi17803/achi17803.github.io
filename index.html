Achintya Bhaduri

BS-MS Chemistry · IISER Bhopal

I am a BS-MS student in Chemistry at IISER Bhopal, interested in understanding how the behaviour of molecules and materials emerges from their underlying physical principles—and what happens when those principles meet real systems.

My work so far has taken me from synthetic chemistry and ultrafast spectroscopy to functional nanomaterials and devices. Across these different systems, I have become particularly interested in using experiments to connect molecular and material properties with phenomena at larger scales, and in how that understanding can be used to engineer new functionality.

More broadly, I am interested in research at the intersection of physical chemistry, materials science, and device physics, particularly in problems where fundamental understanding can open paths toward new materials and functional technologies.
